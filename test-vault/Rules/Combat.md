# Combat

Combat happens in rounds of about six seconds. Everyone rolls initiative once, then acts in order.

## Actions

On your turn you can move and take one action.

### Attack

Make one melee or ranged attack. Roll a d20, add your attack bonus, and compare against the target's Armor Class.

### Dash

Gain extra movement equal to your speed for this turn.

### Disengage

Your movement does not provoke opportunity attacks for the rest of the turn.

## Reactions

A reaction is an instant response to a trigger. You get one per round.

When a hostile creature you can see leaves your reach, you can make one melee attack against it. ^opportunity

> [!tip] Positioning
> Standing in a doorway forces enemies to come to you one at a time.

## Conditions

### Prone

- You can only crawl, or spend half your movement to stand.
- Attacks against you from within 5 feet have advantage.
- Your own attack rolls have disadvantage. ^prone-effects

### Grappled

Your speed becomes 0. The condition ends if the grappler is incapacitated.
